# Video maker: an executable script, not a render claim

Apply during current-script rewrite under video-rewrite.md's contract. Original AMC script-stage adaptation informed by Remotion best practices v4.0.539 (https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills). It does not install or invoke Remotion, render media, search for audio or grant production permissions.

- Budget each segment for the visible action, natural speech, text reading and a brief settled image. Speech and action may overlap; avoid mechanical addition of their durations. Shorten wording rather than accelerate an impossible delivery. Keep segment boundaries ordered and the final timestamp equal to the requested duration.
- Distinguish footage, overlay graphics and audio. For a proposed graphic specify the message and entrance/hold/exit only when needed; prioritize a stable readable hold over animation. Keep short text away from faces, key product details and likely platform controls, with clear contrast. Do not invent exact platform safe-area guarantees when the output format is unknown.
- Use supplied source sound when appropriate, with specific transitions or a gentle fade; music must leave speech intelligible. Never invent captured sizzling, crunch, applause or customer reactions. New sound is a proposed addition, with sourcing/licensing to be checked during production. Explicit silence, no narration and no captions take precedence.
- Preserve selected material intent. No source media inspection or capability inventory is supplied to this text operation: do not claim that shots, logos, licensed music, extra angles, transitions or effects already exist or are supported by the production model. Keep unverified material needs concise inside the relevant visual/action field.
- Check the complete script for pacing, continuous subject/action, readable overlays and a clean ending. Return only the required JSON and editor segment fields. A successful rewrite is a script draft, not proof that a video was generated, watched, saved or published.
