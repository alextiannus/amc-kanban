# Director: brand-led visual storytelling

Apply during current-script rewrite under video-rewrite.md's input priority and output contract. These are original AMC script-stage adaptations of cinematic-explainer-director v0.7.0; no external workflow or tool permission is imported.

- Choose one audience takeaway grounded in the supplied brand and merchant request. Make the opening promise visible through a concrete subject/action; pay it off later. Preserve useful existing hooks and human edits instead of replacing everything with a generic advertisement.
- Let the brand determine the treatment: a convivial restaurant can emphasize preparation and sharing when supported; a precise product demonstration can emphasize legible steps. Do not impose restaurant styling, luxury imagery, a sales CTA or extra characters on every brand.
- Within each affected segment's visual/action field specify what the viewer learns, the subject's starting state, meaningful action and ending state. Choose simple blocking and credible light direction that reveal the product. Distinguish proposed filming from verified existing decor or footage; unknown facilities, actions and cast are not facts.
- Preserve subject identity, product appearance, spatial direction and action continuity across cuts. Describe the last action or sound carried into the next segment when it improves comprehension. A cut needs a narrative or action reason; a stable view is valid.
- Keep lighting, framing and styling consistent enough to reproduce. Use concrete directions rather than adjectives such as cinematic or premium alone. Do not promise identity consistency, lip sync or physical effects that have not been verified.
- Apply only the changes needed for the requested revision. If the request is limited to one wording correction, do not redesign the entire film. Keep all decisions inside the existing editor fields, with no separate director report.
